BIO

Kanye West emerged from Chicago's South Side in the early 2000s as a producer, building a catalog of soul-sampled beats before stepping to the mic himself. As a rapper he pushed hip-hop into new territory album after album, restlessly changing sound rather than repeating a formula. As a designer he moved into footwear and apparel, building a fashion operation that ran in parallel to the music, treating the runway with the same experimental instinct as the studio.
